# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Headless Next.js 15 (App Router, `output: standalone`) on `@latinovation/cms-client` (WebProAI / Latinovation CMS, project slug `velora`), Docker image deployed on Coolify at `velora.webproai.tech`. Same pattern as the TusPlatos storefront (`../tienda-bienestar`).

## Users

Business owners and marketing leads anywhere in the Spanish-speaking world (the agency is Mexican but sells internationally) who need more clients and a steady marketing operation without hiring a full in-house team. They arrive from ads, referrals or search, skim on a phone, and decide whether to book a diagnostic call.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for **Velora**, a marketing agency whose work is executed by AI agents supervised by human strategists. The page sells the service as if it were already a mature product and captures leads (diagnostic requests) into the CMS. Success: a visitor understands what the agents do, trusts that it is real, and leaves their contact.

It is also a live test of the CMS: the Velora team will edit and add content from `/portal`, so every visible value must be editable.

## Positioning

A team of specialised agents (content, ads, WhatsApp sales, sites/SEO/reports) that works 24/7 on the client's marketing, with humans setting strategy and approving. Faster and more constant than a traditional agency, more accountable than a freelancer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services (all four, editable as a collection so the team can add more): content & social, paid media (Meta/Google), WhatsApp attention & sales, sites + SEO + reports.
- Leads: form stored in the CMS only (no email notification for now).
- 100 % editable from `/portal`; editability audit ≥ 95 %.
- Language: neutral Spanish (Mexican origin, international audience). No Colombian-only references.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real clients, testimonials, logos, metrics or pricing were provided. Anything of that kind on the page is demo content clearly owned by the team to replace in the portal; do not present invented third-party brands as real customers.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work, not adjectives: phones, dashboards and pages the agents produce.
2. Agents do, humans decide: always present human oversight.
3. One clear action: book a diagnostic.
4. Everything the visitor sees is a CMS field the team can change.
