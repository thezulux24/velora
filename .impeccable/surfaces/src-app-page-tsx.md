# Velora · landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: business owners and marketing leads (Spanish-speaking, international) deciding whether to book a diagnostic. Action: request a diagnostic via the `diagnostico` form (leads stored in the CMS). Proof: the agents' work rendered on phones and pages (demo content, editable). Constraint: 100 % editable in /portal, audit ≥ 95 %.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Velora is a street of commercial signs that never close. Each agent is a hand-lettered sign whose neon tube ignites when it works. Refuses the near-black AI page with a glowing gradient and the cream editorial page.

OWN-WORLD: Painted walls as section grounds, flat and saturated: rosa mexicano, cobalt, maize yellow, whitewash lime, sign-painter ink. Display in a signage face with an inline/shade layer and an ink drop-shadow; neon is a hairline tube that is grey when off and glows in its colour when lit. Enamel-plaque buttons, 1 px pinstripe rules, a red rubber "APROBADO" stamp for human approval. Body in a sturdy grotesk with tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees four agents (content, paid media, WhatsApp sales, sites/SEO/reports) doing real work on phones and pages, learns that a human strategist approves everything, and books a diagnostic.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed rosa wall. Left 7/12: a huge shaded headline, a one-line lede, an ink plaque "Agenda tu diagnóstico" (primary) and a ghost plaque. Right 5/12: a phone in which the WhatsApp agent answers a customer live, with a lit "ABIERTO 24/7" neon tube hanging over it. The top of the cobalt agents wall shows at the bottom edge.

FORM: Rótulo de neón comercial, position 4 of the ordered list; raises: human "APROBADO" stamp moment (from spy-dossier), one-device-pixel pinstripe discipline (from centre-rail). seed:acd64395aa8bf214

SIGNATURE: neon ignition — each agent sign flickers twice and settles lit as it enters the viewport; the stamp lands once on the approval step. Reduced motion: signs render lit, stamp static.
